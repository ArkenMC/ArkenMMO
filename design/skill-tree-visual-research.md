# Skill tree: visual research (A1, 2026-11-26)

The earlier note `docs/research/skill-tree-art.md` was text only. These references were **downloaded to the session scratchpad and viewed**. None is committed, copied or traced; only the technique notes below are used.

## Viewed
| Reference | What it showed | Used as |
|---|---|---|
| [Diablo II Warcry tree](https://diablo.fandom.com/wiki/File:Warcry_Skill_Tree.JPG) | Dark mottled stone behind square stones. A locked skill is the same silhouette in dark grey; a bought one is a bright flat silhouette on a warmer plate. Connectors are dark channels. The silhouette reads at about 40 px with no interior detail. | One mask per skill, state by value. Noisy stone is fine only if its values stay far under the icons. |
| [Diablo IV pre-alpha tree](https://diablo.fandom.com/wiki/File:Skill_Tree-D4.jpg) | A near-black backdrop; one dim shape behind everything; red light only inside the branches. Squares and circles mark node kinds. | Mystery comes from darkness plus one big dim form. Class light lives **inside** the paths. |
| [Guild Wars 2 warrior skill icons](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Warrior_skills) (12, shrunk to 32 and 16 px, also grey) | Painted scenes turn to mush at 16 px. The ones that survive have one large diagonal or round subject, a bright focal shape and a dark rim. | One bold shape per icon, no figures or scenery. |
| [Starfield skill tree](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Starfield_-_skill_tree.png) | Locked icons are flat and low-contrast; the one owned icon is obvious by colour **and** a brighter frame. | Colour only on owned stones, with a frame change too. |
| [MMOCore GUI pack listing](https://mcmodels.net/products/16712/skill-trees-gui-mmocore-pack) | A chest-menu tree: thin outline nodes on a large flat panel look empty and weak. | What to avoid: nodes must fill the slot and sit in painted seats. |
| [MMOCore docs grid](https://docs.phoenixdevt.fr/mmocore/features/skill-trees.html) | The raw 9x6 grid; arrows in the footer. | Layout check only. |
| [Gotland picture stones, museum](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bildstein-Fornsalen_02.jpg) | Unpainted cuts nearly vanish; the same kind of cut filled with red pigment reads from across the room. Bands of geometry frame the stone. | The core idea: locked = bare cut, owned = pigment in the cut. Faint backdrop carvings stay unpainted. |
| [Hunninge picture stone](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Picture_Stone_from_Hunninge,_Klinte,_Gotland_(29630060325).jpg) | Light figures on a dark ground read as pure silhouettes; lattice border bands feel ancient without any writing. | Margin bands made of invented stave cuts between two rails. |
| [Runsten Uppland 80](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Runsten_Uppland_80.jpg), [Uppland 35 close-up](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Runsten_Uppland_35_closeuo.jpg) | Weathered line carving: a thin dark line with a lighter edge; large quiet stone between lines; long branching cracks cross the art. Traces of red stay in the grooves. | Faint carving recipe, sparse cracks, a trace of old red in the backdrop relief. |
| [Pettigrew prayer rock](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Prayer_Rock_Face_at_Pettigrew_Home_and_Museum_01.jpg) | A shallow carving is found only after looking for it. | The upper limit for backdrop contrast. |
| [Slynyrd, Pixelblog 47](https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2023/11/26/pixelblog-47-tiny-pixels) (sheet 131, rocks) | Rock is built as flat base, one shadow shape, a deeper one and a lit edge, with the hue drifting cool into shadow. | Three close tones per stone surface, plum `ember_stone` sinking into blue `iron`. |

Text read: [Lospec outlines, part 2](https://lospec.com/articles/pixel-art-outlines-part-2-using-color) (outline darker than both neighbours; lighter on lit sides); [League of Legends clarity article](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-au/news/dev/clarity-in-league/) (silhouette first; the most important thing draws the most attention).

Not reachable: wynncraft.wiki.gg and warcraft.wiki.gg returned 403, so no Wynncraft or WoW screenshot was viewed. No Path of Exile image was found. No tutorial specific to pixel-art stone carving was found; the sunken-cut rule rests on the research note's source and on the photos above.
